ABOUT US

Introduction

We are Group 2, a team of BSCS 2B students drawn together by a shared love for storytelling, interactive media, and worlds that linger long after the screen fades.

TALAYA: A Web Visual Novel was born from the quiet intersection of Filipino mythology, modern urban fantasy, and emotional visual novels. It is a story where myths do not remain in the past—they breathe within the streets of present-day Manila, walking unseen beneath city lights and rainy skies.

At its heart, this project is about humanity in its most fragile form—emotions that are too heavy to name, hope that persists through silence, and endings that arrive no matter how tightly we hold on. It exists in a world painted with melancholic beauty: rain-soaked nights, dim reflections on glass, dreamlike distortions of reality, and the soft ache of human connection.

Through TALAYA, we seek to reimagine Filipino mythology not as distant legend, but as something living—woven into everyday life, shaped by memory, emotion, and choice. It is a journey through loneliness and meaning, where every decision carries weight, and every bond leaves a trace.

More than a visual novel, this is an exploration of what it means to feel, to lose, and to still continue forward—even when the ending is already written in the stars.

Our Vision

Where Myth Meets Choice

TALAYA was created with a simple intention—to turn emotion into experience, and storytelling into something that responds, shifts, and remembers.

At its core, this visual novel explores the fragile space between mythology and modern life. Inspired by Filipino legends and reimagined within the streets of present-day, it asks what it means for the past to exist alongside the present—quietly, invisibly, and sometimes painfully.

But beyond mythology, TALAYA is a story about people.

About emotions that are difficult to name.
About loneliness that lingers in silence.
About hope that survives even in endings that feel inevitable.

Through interactive storytelling, we wanted players not just to witness the narrative, but to shape it—where every choice carries weight, and every connection leaves a trace on the world and its characters.

This project is also a reflection of our growth as students of BSCS 2B—learning how systems, art, and narrative can merge into a single experience that feels alive, responsive, and meaningful.

In the end, TALAYA is not just about myths or choices.
It is about finding meaning in what remains… even after the story ends.
