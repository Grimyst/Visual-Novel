A Story Where Your Decisions Matter

What is Talaya?

What is TALAYA? TALAYA is a modern Filipino mythological fantasy set beneath restless skies filled with neon lights, endless rain, and forgotten myths that continue to linger within the modern world. In this story, celestial beings walk quietly among ordinary people, hiding behind familiar faces while carrying memories, emotions, and destinies far older than humanity itself.

At its heart, TALAYA explores the fragile nature of human emotion—loneliness that settles into silence, memories that refuse to fade, and hope that continues to exist even in the face of inevitable endings. Through quiet moments and emotional encounters, the story focuses on the connections people form and the traces they leave behind in one another’s lives.

Through interactive storytelling and meaningful choices, players shape the narrative as relationships evolve and hidden truths slowly reveal themselves. Every decision carries emotional weight, influencing not only the paths of the characters, but also the way the world itself is understood.

Inspired by Filipino mythology, emotional visual novels, and modern urban fantasy, TALAYA combines dreamlike visuals, melancholic atmosphere, and psychological storytelling into an experience that feels both intimate and otherworldly. More than a story about myths, it is a reflection on humanity, emotion, and finding meaning beneath eclipsed skies.

About Us

We are Group 2, a team of BSCS 2B students drawn together by a shared love for storytelling, interactive media, and worlds that linger long after the screen fades. TALAYA:

A Web Visual Novel was born from the quiet intersection of Filipino mythology, modern urban fantasy, and emotional visual novels. It is a story where myths do not remain in the past—they breathe within the streets of present-day Manila, walking unseen beneath city lights and rainy skies. At its heart,

this project is about humanity in its most fragile form—emotions that are too heavy to name, hope that persists through silence, and endings that arrive no matter how tightly we hold on. It exists in a world painted with melancholic beauty: rain-soaked nights, dim reflections on glass, dreamlike distortions of reality, and the soft ache of human connection.

Through TALAYA, we seek to reimagine Filipino mythology not as distant legend, but as something living—woven into everyday life, shaped by memory, emotion, and choice. It is a journey through loneliness and meaning, where every decision carries weight, and every bond leaves a trace. More than a visual novel, this is an exploration of what it means to feel, to lose, and to still continue forward—even when the ending is already written in the stars.
