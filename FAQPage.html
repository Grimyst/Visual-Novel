FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

  • What is TALAYA?

    TALAYA is a modern Filipino mythology-inspired visual novel that combines psychological mystery, supernatural fantasy, emotional storytelling, and interactive choices.

  • What is the story about?

    The story follows individuals whose lives become connected to forgotten gods, repeating memories, and a reality slowly beginning to unravel.

  • What genre is TALAYA?

    TALAYA combines:

    • Psychological Mystery

    • Supernatural Fantasy

    • Filipino Mythology

    • Drama

    • Slice of Life

    • Interactive Narrative

  • Why is it called “TALAYA”?

    The title symbolizes guidance, freedom, destiny, and the invisible connections between people and fate.

  • Is TALAYA based on Filipino mythology?

    Yes. The visual novel is heavily inspired by Philippine mythology and folklore.

  • Is the mythology accurate?

    The story respects the original myths while taking creative liberties for storytelling purposes.

  • Why did you choose Filipino mythology?

    We wanted to showcase Filipino culture and mythology through a modern interactive medium.

  • Where does the story take place?

    The story takes place in a modern Philippine-inspired urban setting blended with surreal and mythological elements.

  • Is TALAYA horror?

    Not entirely. It contains psychological and unsettling moments but focuses more on mystery and emotional storytelling.

  • Is there romance in the game?

    Yes, although romance is not the main focus of the story.

  • Is TALAYA choice-based?

    Yes. Player choices affect scenes, relationships, and endings.

  • Are there multiple endings?

    Yes. Different choices can lead to different outcomes and routes.

  • How long is the game?

    The estimated playtime is around 3–5 hours depending on player choices.

  • Is TALAYA a school project?

    Yes. TALAYA was developed as a collaborative school project.

  • What are the main themes of TALAYA?

    The story explores:

    • Fate versus free will

    • Identity

    • Memory

    • Human connection

    • Hope

    • Grief

    • The fear of being forgotten

  • Who is the protagonist?

    The protagonist is an ordinary individual drawn into a world shaped by forgotten memories and divine influence.

  • Why does reality feel strange in the story?

    Because reality itself is tied to hidden memories and unresolved truths.

  • Are the gods villains?

    No. The gods are portrayed as complex beings with emotions, flaws, and personal struggles.

  • Why are memories important in the story?

    Memories symbolize identity and existence within the narrative.

  • Is the story connected to destiny?

    Yes. Destiny and the struggle against predetermined paths are major themes.

  • Are there hidden meanings and symbolism?

    Yes. Colors, dialogue, names, and environments contain symbolic meaning tied to the story.

    • About Mayari

  • Who is Mayari?

    Mayari is inspired by the moon goddess from Philippine mythology.

  • What is Mayari’s personality?

    Mayari is calm, observant, intelligent, and emotionally reserved.

  • Why is Mayari associated with the moon?

    The moon symbolizes silence, reflection, and hidden emotions, which mirror her character.

  • What role does Mayari play in the story?

    Mayari is deeply connected to the mystery surrounding memory and fate.

    • About Sidapa

  • Who is Sidapa?

    Sidapa is inspired by the Visayan deity associated with death and mortality.

  • Is Sidapa evil?

    No. Sidapa is portrayed as a morally complex character rather than a traditional villain.

  • Why does Sidapa seem distant?

    His character reflects themes of loneliness, inevitability, and mortality.

  • Why is Sidapa important?

    Sidapa challenges characters to confront truths they fear facing.

    • About Libulan

  • Who is Libulan?

    Libulan is inspired by a moon deity from Philippine mythology.

  • What is Libulan’s personality?

    Libulan is expressive, emotional, empathetic, and compassionate.

  • Why is Libulan important?

    Libulan represents emotional vulnerability and the human side of divine existence.

  • Is Libulan connected to Mayari?

    Yes. Their connection symbolizes balance, duality, and emotional contrast.

    • About Hanan

  • Who is Hanan?

    Hanan is inspired by the goddess of dawn and beginnings.

  • What does Hanan symbolize?

    Hanan symbolizes hope, rebirth, healing, and new beginnings.

  • Why is Hanan important to the story?

    Hanan represents the possibility of breaking cycles and creating change.




  • Who developed TALAYA?

    TALAYA was developed collaboratively by students with different roles in programming, writing, illustration, and design.

  • What inspired the visual style?

    The visual style was inspired by atmospheric anime visuals, dreamlike environments, cinematic lighting, and Filipino cultural aesthetics.

  • Did you use royalty-free assets?

    Yes. Certain backgrounds, music, and resources were sourced from royalty-free or properly credited materials.

  • Why did you choose a visual novel format?

    Because visual novels allow emotional storytelling through dialogue, visuals, music, and player interaction.

  • What was the hardest part of development?

    The hardest parts included writing branching dialogue, programming systems, maintaining story consistency, and managing time.

  • What makes TALAYA unique?

    Its blend of Filipino mythology, emotional storytelling, psychological themes, and immersive atmosphere.

  • Are there mini-games?

    Yes. TALAYA includes interactive mini-games that help immerse players in the experience.

  • Does the game have voice acting?

    The game focuses mainly on written dialogue, music, and atmosphere rather than full voice acting.

  • Can player choices affect relationships?

    Yes. Choices can influence character trust, emotional closeness, and story progression.

  • Is there a true ending?

    The story contains endings that reveal deeper truths about the narrative.

  • Can the game be replayed?

    Yes. Multiple routes and endings encourage replayability.

  • Is TALAYA linear?

    No. While the core narrative remains connected, player choices affect scenes and outcomes.

  • Why is this project important?

    TALAYA promotes Filipino culture and mythology while showcasing creativity, programming, storytelling, and multimedia development skills.

  • What is the overall goal of TALAYA?

    The goal of TALAYA is to create an emotional and memorable interactive experience that celebrates Filipino mythology through modern storytelling.
