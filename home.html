What is Talaya?

Talaya is a term coined from the words Tala, the stars but could also be interpreted as fate, and Laya, which means freedom. Hence, the word, Talaya, is the freedom of the user to choose their own challenges, fate, and ending which you hold.

The story will be told through a visual novel format. It is the reimagining of childhood tales told in a modern world, intertwining their origins to create a new, but nostalgic plot. You, as the Bathala, can dictate how your life will unfold. Your destiny lies in your hands, accompanied by the consequences that lie within.

Just like in life, you can unlock different aspects in the Route Map. Soar through the dialogues with the use of the Skip/Auto-play modes. Reflect on the decisions you made through the use of Backlog. Look back on the memories you had made in Chapter Select, a catalogue of specific routes you took, or see whose bonds are forming in the Relationship Charts.
