SETTING

Mayari - Prologue

Rainy Train Station

(Urban Train Station During Stormy Night)

A crowded metropolitan train station during a heavy Philippine monsoon night where rain endlessly crashes against glass windows and steel railings. Neon advertisements, jeepney headlights, and convenience store signs reflect across flooded streets outside while commuters wait beneath flickering fluorescent lights. Distorted station announcements echo through the platforms as trains rumble like distant thunder beneath the city. The atmosphere feels cold, restless, and emotionally isolating, especially once the entire station suddenly loses power and darkness consumes the crowd.

Mayari - Chapter 1

The Girl Who Photographed the Night

(University Photography Darkroom)

A hidden photography darkroom tucked inside an old university arts building where the scent of developing chemicals, damp paper, and dust lingers heavily in the air. Strings of moon photographs hang from wires overhead while rainwater softly taps against narrow windows near the ceiling. The room feels intimate and quiet at first, but the flickering lights, freezing wind, and moving shadows slowly transform the darkroom into something eerie and supernatural.

Mayari - Chapter 2A/2B

Eclipse Dreams

(High Rooftop Above the City)

A rooftop overlooking a sprawling Philippine city skyline at night where distant traffic lights, apartment windows, and storm clouds glow beneath silver moonlight. Cold winds move between tall buildings while the city below continues normally, unaware of the supernatural events unfolding above. Despite the beautiful nighttime view, the rooftop feels emotionally distant and lonely, becoming colder and darker as Mayari reveals her connection to the moon.

Mayari - Chapter 3A/3B

The Sea Below the City

(Coastal Bayfront and Flooded Shoreline)

A coastal urban shoreline transformed into a supernatural disaster zone beneath a crimson eclipse. Massive ocean waves rise unnaturally toward the sky while floodwaters consume roads, seawalls, and nearby buildings. Thunderstorms rage above the coastline as the familiar bay becomes something terrifying and mythological, blurring the line between natural disaster and divine catastrophe.

Mayari - Final Chapter

The Eclipse

(Collapsing Rooftop During the Apocalypse)

A ruined rooftop standing above a flooded and partially frozen city beneath a shattered crimson moon. Rain hangs motionless in midair while gigantic walls of ocean surround the skyline like prison barriers beneath the eclipse. Buildings tremble, storm clouds twist unnaturally, and supernatural light spreads across the heavens, creating an atmosphere that feels both apocalyptic and strangely beautiful.

Libulan - Prologue

The City That Couldn't Feel

(Underground Nightclub During a Storm)

A crowded underground nightclub hidden beneath the busy streets of a Philippine city during a violent rainstorm. Neon lights flash endlessly across smoke, spilled drinks, and tightly packed crowds while bass vibrations shake the walls hard enough to rattle the ceiling. Outside, floodwaters rise through dark streets beneath heavy rain, but inside the club emotions are manipulated through music, turning the atmosphere euphoric, chaotic, and emotionally overwhelming until panic suddenly breaks loose.

The Man Immune to Music

(Hospital Cafeteria Late at Night)

A quiet hospital cafeteria illuminated by harsh fluorescent lighting while exhausted nurses, patients, and night-shift workers move silently through nearby hallways. The air smells faintly of coffee, antiseptic, and rain-soaked clothing while vending machines hum softly in the background. The setting feels emotionally drained and sterile, becoming increasingly unsettling once the strange silver countdowns above people begin freezing in place.

The Morning That Repeated

(Morning Streets and Train Station at Sunrise)

An early morning cityscape filled with soft sunrise light reflecting across wet roads, jeepneys, train stations, convenience stores, and sleepy commuters beginning another workday. Everything initially feels ordinary and comforting, but repeating details—the same flickering streetlight, the same spilled coffee, the same stray cat—make the city feel trapped inside an endless loop. Once time freezes completely, the familiar morning atmosphere becomes eerie, nostalgic, and deeply unnatural.
