PLOT

In this visual novel, a rain-soaked modern city slowly begins unraveling beneath endless eclipses, distorted time, and strange phenomena no ordinary person can fully explain. What first appears to be an ordinary urban setting soon reveals itself as a world where ancient Filipino myths continue existing beneath neon lights, crowded streets, and sleepless nights. As reality itself starts collapsing through frozen mornings, disappearing emotions, and skies consumed by darkness, the boundary between humanity and mythology becomes increasingly fragile.

At the center of the story are four individuals whose lives become deeply connected to the fate of the city itself. Mayari is a quiet and mysterious girl tied to the moon and its fading light, carrying loneliness hidden behind silver eyes and silence. Hanan is an energetic early riser who finds comfort in routines and continues believing in second chances despite being trapped in repeating mornings that never seem to properly begin. Sidapa is a composed hospital worker burdened with the ability to see the exact moment people will die, causing him to distance himself emotionally from others. Meanwhile, Libulan is a charismatic DJ capable of controlling emotions through music while secretly struggling with his own inability to genuinely connect with people around him.

As the ancient serpent Bakunawa awakens beneath the city and begins spreading despair through eclipses, silence, and collapsing time, each character is forced to confront their own fears, emotional wounds, and understanding of humanity itself. Through interactive choices and emotionally driven storytelling, the visual novel explores themes of mortality, loneliness, hope, identity, and human connection while reimagining Filipino mythology within a modern urban fantasy setting filled with rain, moonlight, music, and the lingering feeling that the world may already be approaching its end.
